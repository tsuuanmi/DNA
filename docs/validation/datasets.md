# Validation Datasets and Evidence

DNA distinguishes synthetic repository fixtures from approved real-world validation evidence.

## Synthetic evidence

Synthetic ABIF and deterministic fixtures may be committed when they contain no identifying biological data. They protect parser, coordinate, algorithm, schema, and regression boundaries.

## Real AB1 evidence

Real traces may be used only under [data governance](../governance/data.md). Release evidence must preserve provenance sufficient to reproduce the evaluation while keeping sensitive source data out of the repository unless explicitly approved.

## Ground truth

Agreement with another implementation is not automatically truth. Ground truth or reviewer-established expectations must be independently defined for the claim being validated.

Dataset changes that alter release claims require review of acceptance criteria and traceability.
