# Normalized Variant Aggregation

Part of the canonical [sample evidence aggregation method](README.md).

Canonical normalized variant observations are separately grouped by `(position, reference, alternate, kind)`. Each support publishes the unique reviewer-facing read name plus configured eligibility, exclusion reasons, and reference-oriented base/peak/quality evidence. For every normalized variant, DNA also derives factorized support topology from those same observations: total/eligible observing reads and forward/reverse plus eligible-forward/eligible-reverse counts. The summary never adds reference-supporting or unresolved coverage as variant support and is not a confidence or vote. Internal aggregation remains SHA-ordered and index-based, but numeric indexes do not leak into the reviewer contract. A read-level filter can remove a candidate from `analysis/v9` reporting without erasing the observation from `SampleEvidence`. Insertions are normalized variant evidence rather than fabricated reference-locus observations. No consensus or sample-level conflict verdict is produced in v10; coverage topology and overlap eligibility are pre-consensus evidence only.

Every normalized variant also records its opposition (ADR-0068): the admitted reads whose callable reference segments cover the variant's evidence span and that have no support record for it, in read-registry order with forward/reverse counts. The evidence span is the anchored reference allele — the position of an SNV, the anchor and deleted bases of a deletion — and, for an insertion, the anchor and the base after it. A read that supports a different event there opposes this variant too. Positions past the reference end are never callable, so a span that would cross the end of a circular reference has no opposition. Opposition is evidence only; it is never combined with support into a vote, weight, or verdict.
