# Signal-derived Read Callability Requirements

**Requirement namespace:** `SRS-CALL-*`

These requirements are part of the canonical [[requirements/SRS|DNA SRS]].

- **SRS-CALL-001:** DNA MUST derive, for every read and without reference, target-profile, primer, or file-name knowledge, one per-position callability record in trace order from the immutable analyzed channels, canonical loci, basecall-independent `LocusEvidence`, and primary calls. The derivation MUST NOT modify channels, loci, calls, selected peaks, or locus evidence.
- **SRS-CALL-002:** Per-position features MUST follow the documented `dna.read_callability/v1` formulas — primary/secondary channel ranking, dominance, secondary ratio, spacing deviation from the local median spacing, and weakness relative to the read's median primary amplitude — with six-decimal rounding before storage or comparison. Every segmentation and classification threshold MUST be configuration (SRS-CFG-008), including the spacing defect threshold (`callability.spacing_defect`), the spacings on each side of a position whose median anchors its spacing deviation (`callability.spacing_half_window`), the double-peak ratio (`callability.double_peak_ratio`), and the calls after a repeat run that form its prior window (`callability.repeat_window_calls`); the shadow model's offset range of three calls, its minimum fit size (one more than its parameters), and the solver tolerances are the model's definition and documented method constants.
- **SRS-CALL-003:** Phase segmentation MUST assign every call position exactly one state from the closed set `in_phase`, `dephased`, `mixed`, `weak`, `irregular`, as ordered 0-based half-open call intervals that partition `[0, call_count)`. A masked stretch MAY return to `in_phase` after it; a callable island shorter than one window MUST be absorbed by its masked neighbours. A masked segment dominated by double peaks MUST be `dephased` exactly when the documented shadow model — a deterministic non-negative least-squares fit of its normalized amplitudes to the read's own primary calls shifted by −3..+3 calls — reaches the configured main-ladder share, stays within the configured far-shadow share, and carries a one-call shadow at or above the configured shadow share; otherwise it MUST be `mixed`. Classification MUST NOT move segment boundaries.
- **SRS-CALL-004:** Repeat runs of at least `callability.repeat_min_length` identical or alternating canonical primary calls MAY lower the onset threshold in the window that follows the run and MUST attribute a segment that starts in that window (`after_repeat`), but MUST NOT mask by themselves or lower any per-position threshold inside the run. A masked stretch whose first defect lies in the window after a run MUST start right after the run.
- **SRS-CALL-005:** The mask MUST mark every position of a non-`in_phase` segment; the callable span MUST be the interval from the first to the last unmasked position and MUST be empty when every position is masked. A published `dephased` segment MUST list its reported shadow offsets in ascending order; no other segment carries them.
- **SRS-CALL-006:** Operational logs MUST record callability as aggregate metrics (repeat and segment counts per state, masked-call count, callable span, one bounded token per segment) and MUST NOT emit per-position arrays.
- **SRS-CALL-007:** Phase states, mask reasons, callability features, and shadow-model weights or shares MUST NOT be described as Phred, error probability, heteroplasmy, mixture fraction, or artifact classification; shadow weights and shares MUST NOT be published in result contracts.
- **SRS-CALL-008:** The callability core MUST operate on plain per-position records; decoding Sanger types into those records MUST live in one adapter that owns no thresholds, and no modality trait is introduced until a second adapter exists.
- **SRS-CALL-009:** A read with fewer than `callability.minimum_callable_calls` unmasked calls MUST fail `analyze` and `basecall` with a typed callability error after its callability is logged; in `sample` it MUST be recorded as rejected instead (SRS-SAMPLE-027).
- **SRS-CALL-010:** Quality control MUST derive the trim interval from the callable span (SRS-QC-004).
- **SRS-CALL-011:** Alignment MUST present every masked call inside the informative interval as an unresolved query base without an evidence profile, except a `dephased` call, which MUST keep its call and profile; masked query bases MUST be counted separately from unresolved ones (SRS-ALN-015).
- **SRS-CALL-012:** A masked call MUST NOT be an SNV candidate, and a masked evidence call of another variant MUST make it ineligible with the reason of its segment (SRS-VAR-013); a masked observation at a sample locus MUST NOT retain the locus by itself (SRS-SAMPLE-024).
- **SRS-CALL-013:** The Sanger evidence MUST place at an edge of the read's usable signal, for its base and for indels, every call within `sanger_evidence.signal_edge_margin` calls of a call outside the trim interval, of a masked call that does not anchor the alignment, or of a physical read end, judged before SRS-CALL-014, SRS-CALL-017, and SRS-CALL-018 apply. The core MUST read only these verdicts for the signal edges (SRS-VAR-013).
- **SRS-CALL-014:** Before the callable span, a masked call MUST be vouched for by its base only (scored, its bound withheld) when it is not masked after a repeat run (`post_homopolymer`, the read's dephased tail rather than start noise), its primary is canonical, no other channel qualifies, and its primary peak is at least `sanger_evidence.leading_height_fraction` of the median primary peak of the read's canonical calls. Such a call MUST be unmasked and not at an edge, MUST keep the peak-floor reason but not the relative-quality reason, and the informative interval MUST start at the first such call. The rule MUST judge each call against its own read only, never a reference position, primer, or file name: a read starts with noise its masked stretch only leaves where a whole window is clean, so strong, unambiguous calls inside that stretch are masked with it.
- **SRS-CALL-015:** The Sanger evidence MUST give every call with a canonical primary its second base: the highest peak of the other three channels, with its share of that peak and the primary peak together, when that peak is positive. The second base is evidence for review only; no calling rule reads it, and it MUST NOT be described as a heteroplasmy fraction (SRS-CALL-007).
- **SRS-CALL-016:** The Sanger evidence MUST mark a run of at least two calls of one canonical base, with its two bounds, as a bounded run before a post-repeat phase loss (ADR-0076) when every call of the run and its bounds is scored and counts towards runs, each bound shows a run's end and reads another base, on each side either more than `sanger_evidence.post_repeat_margin` informative calls separate the bound from the nearest uninformative call or at least `sanger_evidence.post_repeat_minimum_calls` do and that call is masked in a segment that starts right after a repeat run, and some call of the run or its bounds lies at a signal edge or within the margin of the read's ends. The rule MUST read the read's own calls only; a read's physical end, the trim interval, or any other masked segment never marks a run.
- **SRS-CALL-017:** Within `sanger_evidence.edge_identity_margin` calls of an end of the read's informative interval, a scored call that SRS-CALL-013 places at a signal edge MUST have its verdict on its base taken off the edge when its own signal is unambiguous: a canonical primary with no other qualifying channel, a primary peak at least `sanger_evidence.edge_height_fraction` of the read's median primary peak, a second-channel share below `sanger_evidence.edge_second_share`, a primary peak no wider at half its amplitude than `sanger_evidence.edge_width_factor` times the read's median width, and a spacing to each neighbouring call within `sanger_evidence.edge_spacing_minimum` to `sanger_evidence.edge_spacing_maximum` times the read's median spacing. Its verdict on an indel MUST keep the edge and its bound MUST not change, so it still counts towards runs as before. It MUST keep the peak-floor reason but not the relative-quality reason. Only a call qualifies when every call between it and that end, and every call within the margin on its inner side, is scored; when no call within the margin beyond that end is masked where the read loses phase (`dephased_signal`, `post_homopolymer`), unless the informative interval ends with a run of one canonical base, at least two calls, that starts after the call and the first call beyond that end is masked `post_homopolymer` (the read loses phase after that run, and before it every molecule shows the same sequence); and when it does not read the base of its neighbour towards that end, which a signal one position off would. The rule MUST judge each call against its own read only, never a reference position, primer, or file name.
- **SRS-CALL-018:** At the read's start, up to `sanger_evidence.start_identity_margin` calls past the start of the callable span, a scored call that SRS-CALL-013 places at a signal edge MUST have its verdict on its base taken off the edge, as in SRS-CALL-017, when its own signal is unambiguous against the start's own norms, the median primary peak, width, and spacing of the first `sanger_evidence.start_norm_calls` in-phase single-channel calls of the callable span: a canonical primary with no other qualifying channel, a primary peak at least `sanger_evidence.start_height_fraction` of that median (a read's signal rises over its first calls), a second-channel share below `sanger_evidence.start_second_share` (start noise carries more shadow than a read's end), a peak no wider than `sanger_evidence.edge_width_factor` times that median width, and a spacing to each neighbour within the edge spacing band of SRS-CALL-017 times that median spacing. Masked calls before the callable span are start noise and MAY lie between the call and the read's start; no masked call of the callable span may, every call within the margin on its inner side MUST be scored, and the call MUST NOT read the base of the call before it unless that call is vouched for its base, scored and off the edge or taken off it by this rule, judged in read order (a call reading its predecessor's base may be that call's signal one position off; a vouched predecessor with regular spacing shows two calls of a run). Its verdict on an indel MUST keep the edge and its bound MUST not change. The rule MUST judge each call against its own read only.
- **SRS-CALL-019:** A run signal MUST record, for one run's traces, how the selected calls of every read the sample commands would admit to evidence show each of their other channels: keyed by the read-strand context of the read's own primary calls centred on the call, `signal_processing.run_signal_flank` calls on each side (a 5-mer at 2), the call's base, and one other channel, as counts per share in `signal_processing.run_signal_resolution` steps (thousandths at 1000), the share being that channel's peak against the call's primary peak and itself (for the strongest other channel, the call's second-base share, SRS-CALL-015). Every selected call MUST count under each of its three other channels. A call MUST be selected only when it is informative and scored, its verdict on its base is clear of a signal edge (as SRS-CALL-013, SRS-CALL-017, and SRS-CALL-018 leave it, so a change to those rules changes the run signal) and free of the peak-floor and relative-quality reasons (the mixed reason does not exclude it), and its base and the `run_signal_flank` calls on each side are canonical; a read's first and last `run_signal_flank` calls have no context. The selection MUST be one function used both to build a run signal and to take a read's own contributions out of it, and its version, flank, and resolution MUST be recorded in the run signal; a command that reads a run signal MUST refuse one whose flank or resolution differs from its configuration's. A run signal MUST record the content SHA-256 of every trace of the run and MUST read no reference, alignment, or truth.
- **SRS-CALL-020:** The second-channel threshold of a call MUST be one function of the call's key (SRS-CALL-019) and an optional run signal, on the peak scale of SRS-CALL-015: `signal_processing.second_floor` when there is no run signal or no key (a call within `run_signal_flank` calls of a read end, or without a canonical context), or when the run signal, the sample's own admitted reads taken out, holds fewer than `signal_processing.run_signal_minimum_reads` reads or the key fewer than `signal_processing.run_signal_minimum_calls` calls; otherwise the larger of the floor and the key's run level, `median + max(run_signal_margin, run_signal_mad_factor × MAD)` of the key's shares. A call's second base stands out when its share exceeds the threshold. Every rule that judges whether a call's second base stands out as a possible second sequence MUST use this function and no other threshold (a rule MAY add conditions other than the share, such as the review's neighbour-shadow test); a rule that asks whether a call's own signal is clean, such as SRS-CALL-017 and SRS-CALL-018, is not such a judgement. The function MUST read no reference position, alignment, or truth.
- **SRS-CALL-021:** The Sanger plugin MUST label why a call shows a second peak, from the read's own calls and signal only, never a reference position, alignment, vendor peak position, or truth, and the label MUST decide nothing by itself: no verdict, mask, call, or review item may read it, though a report may show it, and the evidence adapter MUST turn it into the generic assertions of SRS-CALL-022 only. A call MUST carry a label when its second base's share (SRS-CALL-015, the same computation) exceeds `signal_processing.second_floor`, or when its callability state is mixed (`mixed_stretch` if that share does not exceed the floor). The call's centre is its primary channel's selected peak, its spacing half the distance between its neighbours' primary peaks, and its widths the peak widths signal processing measures at its primary event. The first rule that holds MUST give the label: `noise` when the second peak is below `signal_processing.second_cause_noise_ratio` times the call's lowest channel; `neighbour_tail` when the second channel has no local maximum in the call's window, or its peak lies at least `signal_processing.second_cause_tail_offset` spacings off the centre towards a neighbouring call of the second base; `off_centre` when it lies that far off the centre otherwise; `dye_blob` when the second channel is at least `signal_processing.second_cause_blob_width` times as wide as the median primary width of the `signal_processing.second_cause_width_calls` calls on each side; `phase_shift` when the second base is a neighbouring call's base; otherwise `second_template`. Given a run signal, a `second_template` call whose share does not exceed its threshold (SRS-CALL-020) at a usable run level MUST be labelled `context` instead.
- **SRS-CALL-022:** The Sanger evidence MUST assert, per call, what the core may read of its second peak (ADR-0080), with the second base and share of SRS-CALL-015: the second base stands out (`SecondBase.stands_out`) when its share exceeds `signal_processing.second_floor`; the call shows a second peak it does not explain (`second_unexplained`) when SRS-CALL-021 labels it with a cause other than `neighbour_tail` or `dye_blob`; and the call is reference-only (`reference_only`) when its base is withheld as mixed (`mixed_supporting_dna`) and otherwise at most for the peak floor or its relative quality, it is in phase, both neighbouring calls show one base (one qualifying channel, or a second peak that SRS-CALL-021 labels `neighbour_tail` or `dye_blob`: a blob is wider than one call) and are in phase or, before the trim interval, masked as weak, and its cause is `neighbour_tail` or `dye_blob`, unless it lies at a signal edge (SRS-CALL-013) that is not benign as defined below. Once the signal edges are known, a scored call with one qualifying channel and no unexplained second peak (its second base may stand out) MUST also be reference-only when it is withheld only for the peak floor or its relative quality, or lies at a benign signal edge: an edge every cause of which within `sanger_evidence.signal_edge_margin` calls is a call before the trim interval (the untrimmed read start, not its untrimmed 3' end), or a weak-signal mask whose segment lies more than that margin from every repeat run of the read; a read's physical end is never benign. A reference-only call MUST keep its reasons, its edge verdict, its bound, and its run role unchanged. The review MAY read `second_unexplained` to tell a possible heteroplasmy from a second peak the signal's shape explains ([[reference/review|review]], rules version 19); it MUST NOT read the cause itself.
- **SRS-CALL-023:** The Sanger evidence MUST assert a phase mixture (`ReadEvidence::mixtures`, ADR-0082) for every dephased segment that a repeat run opens, from the read's own calls and normalised A/C/G/T profiles only, never a reference position, alignment, or truth. The opening run MUST be the longest maximal run of at least two equal canonical primary calls whose end lies within `callability.repeat_window_calls` calls of the segment's start (the nearer on a tie), and it MUST start at or after the trim interval's start; the stretch MUST start at that run and end at the segment's end, or where the next run of primary calls at least as long as the opening one starts. Over the stretch, the profiles MUST be fitted by non-negative least squares as the read's primary calls superposed with copies of themselves offset by whole calls, no offset longer than the opening run: starting from offset 0 alone, the offset that lowers the residual most MUST be added while it lowers the residual (the root mean square per call) by more than the read's own noise, the root mean square of `profile − onehot(primary)` over its in-phase single-channel calls. A call of the dephased segment whose own residual exceeds the `signal_processing.decompose_unexplained_quantile` nearest-rank quantile of those in-phase residuals MUST be listed as unexplained, and the mixture MUST be explained only when its residual does not exceed that quantile. The components MUST be offset 0 first, then the others by decreasing fraction, the fractions summing to one. A read with fewer in-phase single-channel calls than `callability.minimum_callable_calls` MUST assert no mixture. A mixture MUST decide nothing: no verdict, mask, call, or review item reads it.
- **SRS-CALL-024:** The Sanger evidence MUST assert the read's poor start as an unreliable span (`ReadEvidence::unreliable`, reason `poor_start_signal`, [[PROP-0012]]): from the read's first call to the first run of `sanger_evidence.start_identity_margin` consecutive sound calls, a sound call being scored, clear of a signal edge for its base, and withheld for nothing, judged after the edge and start rules (SRS-CALL-013, SRS-CALL-017, SRS-CALL-018) apply; no span when the read's first calls are such a run, and the whole read when no such run exists. The span MUST change no verdict, mask, or call; the core MUST decide nothing from it except which reportable positions at a region's end are not analysed (SRS-CONS-012, [[ADR-0085]]), and a report MAY show it with its reason.
- **SRS-CALL-025:** The Sanger evidence MUST assert a tract decode (`ReadEvidence::tracts`, [[PROP-0012]], [[ADR-0084]]) before every call where callability sees the read leave phase, the start of a segment not `in_phase`. It MUST use the read's analysed channels at its calls' locus samples, each row divided by its sum (at least one), and never a reference position, alignment, or truth.
  - **Location.** The tract MUST be the latest run of at least `trace.locate_min_run` equal primary calls that starts before that call, extended back over each single call of another base that at least `trace.locate_min_before` run-base calls precede. An onset with no such run MUST yield no tract, and onsets that locate the same first call MUST yield one.
  - **Structure.** The structure MUST be the lowest-residual one among a single run and a mark of each other base, with the earlier in that order winning a tie. Each candidate MUST be fitted with its own run extent and candidate lengths, from `trace.span_below` calls under the extent (at least `trace.min_length`) to `trace.span_above` over it, but all over the same calls: the longest extent plus `trace.span_above` and `trace.downstream_calls`, within the read. A candidate longer than the calls fitted MUST be left out, and a tract none of whose candidates fits MUST yield no decode.
  - **Fit.** Each fit MUST alternate two steps, from a start per candidate length, for at most `trace.iterations` rounds: non-negative least squares over every molecule's fraction, then an exact decode of the shared downstream sequence over at most `trace.joint_lengths` consecutive lengths.
  - **Output.** It MUST publish only the identifiable marginals, the first run's and the total's, each with its standard errors. Each marginal MUST carry the margin of its dominant length over the next, deflated by the residual's lag-1 autocorrelation, and its state: `near_equal` when that margin is below `trace.near_equal_z`, `undefined` when two lengths show and the margin is not finite, and `clear` otherwise. An `undefined` margin MUST never count as clear. It MUST also publish the chosen structure refitted with the read's own base signatures and neighbour leakage, both measured on at most `trace.signature_calls` calls before the tract.
  - **Scope.** A decode MUST decide nothing: no verdict, mask, call, or review item reads it.
- **SRS-CALL-026:** The Sanger evidence MUST vouch for the two calls beside a junction whose spacing marks a missing peak by their bases only (`Bound::Withheld`): such a call places no insertion or deletion and shows no run's end. A junction marks a missing peak when, with `M` the median spacing of the window SRS-CALL-002 anchors at the call before it, the spacing between its two calls' locus positions exceeds `M`, and its span together with each neighbouring junction exceeds `2M`, each by at least `callability.spacing_defect` of `M`: the spacing defect's measure and level, in the wide direction only; no other threshold applies. A basecaller that misses a peak leaves a gap about twice the read's spacing and the ladder a call behind, and the read then shows a deletion the template does not hold; a peak merely placed late is followed by a short spacing that makes up for it, and marks nothing. The rule MUST change no verdict on a base, mask, or reason, and MUST read the read's own locus positions only, never a reference position, alignment, or truth.
