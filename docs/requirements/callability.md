# Signal-derived Read Callability Requirements

**Requirement namespace:** `SRS-CALL-*`

These requirements are part of the canonical [DNA SRS](SRS.md).

- **SRS-CALL-001:** DNA MUST derive, for every read and without reference, target-profile, primer, or file-name knowledge, one per-position callability record in trace order from the immutable analyzed channels, canonical loci, basecall-independent `LocusEvidence`, and primary calls. The derivation MUST NOT modify channels, loci, calls, selected peaks, or locus evidence.
- **SRS-CALL-002:** Per-position features MUST follow the documented `dna.read_callability/v1` formulas — primary/secondary channel ranking, dominance, secondary ratio, spacing deviation from the local median spacing, and weakness relative to the read's median primary amplitude — with six-decimal rounding before storage or comparison. Every segmentation and classification threshold MUST be configuration (SRS-CFG-008); the spacing defect threshold, the shadow offset range of three calls, the minimum fit size, and the solver tolerances are documented method constants.
- **SRS-CALL-003:** Phase segmentation MUST assign every call position exactly one state from the closed set `in_phase`, `dephased`, `mixed`, `weak`, `irregular`, as ordered 0-based half-open call intervals that partition `[0, call_count)`. A masked stretch MAY return to `in_phase` after it; a callable island shorter than one window MUST be absorbed by its masked neighbours. A masked segment dominated by double peaks MUST be `dephased` exactly when the documented shadow model — a deterministic non-negative least-squares fit of its normalized amplitudes to the read's own primary calls shifted by −3..+3 calls — reaches the configured main-ladder share, stays within the configured far-shadow share, and carries a one-call shadow at or above the configured shadow share; otherwise it MUST be `mixed`. Classification MUST NOT move segment boundaries.
- **SRS-CALL-004:** Repeat runs of at least `callability.repeat_min_length` identical or alternating canonical primary calls MAY lower the onset threshold in the window that follows the run and MUST attribute a segment that starts in that window (`after_repeat`), but MUST NOT mask by themselves or lower any per-position threshold inside the run. A masked stretch whose first defect lies in the window after a run MUST start right after the run.
- **SRS-CALL-005:** The mask MUST mark every position of a non-`in_phase` segment; the callable span MUST be the interval from the first to the last unmasked position and MUST be empty when every position is masked. A published `dephased` segment MUST list its reported shadow offsets in ascending order; no other segment carries them.
- **SRS-CALL-006:** Operational logs MUST record callability as aggregate metrics (repeat and segment counts per state, masked-call count, callable span, one bounded token per segment) and MUST NOT emit per-position arrays.
- **SRS-CALL-007:** Phase states, mask reasons, callability features, and shadow-model weights or shares MUST NOT be described as Phred, error probability, heteroplasmy, mixture fraction, or artifact classification; shadow weights and shares MUST NOT be published in result contracts.
- **SRS-CALL-008:** The callability core MUST operate on plain per-position records; decoding Sanger types into those records MUST live in one adapter that owns no thresholds, and no modality trait is introduced until a second adapter exists.
- **SRS-CALL-009:** A read with fewer than `callability.minimum_callable_calls` unmasked calls MUST fail `analyze` and `basecall` with a typed callability error after its callability is logged; in `sample` it MUST be recorded as rejected instead (SRS-SAMPLE-027).
- **SRS-CALL-010:** Quality control MUST derive the trim interval from the callable span (SRS-QC-004).
- **SRS-CALL-011:** Alignment MUST present every masked call inside the informative interval as an unresolved query base without an evidence profile, except a `dephased` call, which MUST keep its call and profile; masked query bases MUST be counted separately from unresolved ones (SRS-ALN-015).
- **SRS-CALL-012:** A masked call MUST NOT be an SNV candidate, and a masked evidence call of another variant MUST make it ineligible with the reason of its segment (SRS-VAR-013); a masked observation at a sample locus MUST NOT retain the locus by itself (SRS-SAMPLE-024).
- **SRS-CALL-013:** The Sanger evidence MUST place at an edge of the read's usable signal, for its base and for indels, every call within `variant_calling.read_end_margin` calls of a call outside the trim interval, of a masked call that does not anchor the alignment, or of a physical read end, judged before SRS-CALL-014 and SRS-CALL-017 apply. The core MUST read only these verdicts for the signal edges (SRS-VAR-013).
- **SRS-CALL-014:** Before the callable span, a masked call MUST be vouched for by its base only (scored, its bound withheld) when its primary is canonical, no other channel qualifies, and its primary peak is at least the median primary peak of the read's canonical calls. Such a call MUST be unmasked and not at an edge, MUST keep the peak-floor reason but not the relative-quality reason, and the informative interval MUST start at the first such call. The rule MUST judge each call against its own read only, never a reference position, primer, or file name: a read starts with noise its masked stretch only leaves where a whole window is clean, so strong, unambiguous calls inside that stretch are masked with it.
- **SRS-CALL-015:** The Sanger evidence MUST give every call with a canonical primary its second base: the highest peak of the other three channels, with its share of that peak and the primary peak together, when that peak is positive. The second base is evidence for review only; no calling rule reads it, and it MUST NOT be described as a heteroplasmy fraction (SRS-CALL-007).
- **SRS-CALL-016:** The Sanger evidence MUST mark a run of at least two calls of one canonical base, with its two bounds, as a bounded run before a post-repeat phase loss (ADR-0076) when every call of the run and its bounds is scored and counts towards runs, each bound shows a run's end and reads another base, on each side either more than `variant_calling.read_end_margin` informative calls separate the bound from the nearest uninformative call or at least two do and that call is masked in a segment that starts right after a repeat run, and some call of the run or its bounds lies at a signal edge or within the margin of the read's ends. The rule MUST read the read's own calls only; a read's physical end, the trim interval, or any other masked segment never marks a run.
- **SRS-CALL-017:** Within `variant_calling.read_end_margin` calls of an end of the read's informative interval, a scored call that SRS-CALL-013 places at a signal edge MUST have its verdict on its base taken off the edge when its own signal is unambiguous: a canonical primary with no other qualifying channel, a primary peak at least the read's median primary peak, a second-channel share below 0.10, a primary peak no wider at half its amplitude than 1.5 times the read's median width, and a spacing to each neighbouring call within 0.5 to 1.5 times the read's median spacing. Its verdict on an indel MUST keep the edge and its bound MUST not change, so it still counts towards runs as before. It MUST keep the peak-floor reason but not the relative-quality reason. Only a call qualifies when every call between it and that end, and every call within the margin on its inner side, is scored; when no call within the margin beyond that end is masked where the read loses phase (`dephased_signal`, `post_homopolymer`); and when it does not read the base of its neighbour towards that end, which a signal one position off would. The rule MUST judge each call against its own read only, never a reference position, primer, or file name.
