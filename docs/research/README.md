# Research

Research under `docs/research/<topic>/` is exploratory and non-normative.

A research note, prototype design, or research ADR does not change production behavior merely by existing. Promotion into production requires the appropriate root SRS, ADR, method, contract, implementation, test, and validation changes described by [documentation governance](../governance/documentation.md).

Keep topic-specific exploration inside its own subtree rather than adding catch-all notes to the `docs/` root.
